Watch Import

Brings your watch history, Continue Watching and library from Stremio or Nuvio into AIOStreams, and keeps them up to date as you watch there. Nothing is ever written back.

1. Choose where your history is

2. Sign in to Stremio

Approve this page from a browser where you are signed in to Stremio. Works for accounts made with Google or Facebook too.

Open and approve it.

Sent straight to Stremio from this page. This server never sees it.

3. Choose what to bring in

4. Add it to AIOStreams

  1. In AIOStreams, go to Addons → Installed, click Add by URL and paste it.
  2. AIOStreams reads it in the background, and straight away when an app opens Continue Watching or Next Up.
  3. For a one-time import, remove the addon once the Trackers tab shows a successful read. What was imported stays.
